## What are WARDOGS Gold Bars?

Gold Bars are planned as a longer-term or endgame currency in the WARDOGS metagame. Official material connects them with exchanging accumulated Cash and purchasing cosmetics through a planned Gold Market. They are separate from the Cash used for each-life weapons, gear, utility, and vehicles.

This system is part of Early Access planning rather than a confirmed launch-day store. The public sources do not provide a final Gold Bars interface, item catalogue, exchange formula, or real-money policy. Those fields remain 待确认.

## How does Cash become Gold Bars?

The official video describes a planned choice to exchange Cash for Gold Bars during a season. It says the exchange rate is intended to change daily, so the value is not a permanently fixed conversion table. At the end of a season, remaining Cash is planned to convert automatically into Gold Bars.

The exact rate, minimum amount, fee, cap, and conversion timing are not published. A guide can explain the direction of the system but cannot calculate a profitable exchange or promise a specific number of Gold Bars. Treat every formula as 待确认 until the developer releases it.

## What happens to Cash and XP at a season reset?

The collected official planning material says seasonal Cash and XP progression reset, with remaining Cash automatically converted into Gold Bars. Gold Bars and cosmetics are described as persisting across seasons. This creates a distinction between short-term match resources and longer-term account value.

The reset date, handling of unspent items, conversion order, and transfer into version 1.0 are not confirmed. The seasonal plan can also change during Early Access. Keep the wording tied to the official planning source rather than presenting it as a live account rule.

## What is the Gold Market?

The Gold Market is the planned destination for Gold Bars and is associated with cosmetic unlocks. Official sources do not publish its final catalogue, item rotation, purchase limits, or whether every cosmetic uses Gold Bars. It should not be confused with the in-match vendor that sells loadout items.

The same evidence says the developers do not plan to sell in-game Cash or Gold Bars as a core Early Access economy promise. That statement is about the published plan and should be updated if an official policy changes. Real-money purchases, bundles, trading, and regional pricing remain 待确认.

## Are Gold Bars the same as Persistent Cash?

No. Persistent Cash is used to buy the equipment for each life and is earned through combat and teamplay, while Gold Bars are planned as a longer-term account currency. Cash can affect the next match immediately; Gold Bars are linked to seasonal conversion and cosmetic progression.

Keeping the two balances separate prevents incorrect loadout advice. A player should not treat Gold Bars as a replacement for the money needed to buy a vehicle or medical item in the next life. The final relationship between account balances, Vault items, and cosmetics is 待确认.

## How should players plan around Gold Bars?

Use Cash first for the current objective and future lives. If the seasonal exchange system ships as described, players may choose when to convert Cash based on the daily rate and their remaining match needs. Do not sacrifice a necessary loadout simply to chase a planned cosmetic balance.

The public evidence does not support a best exchange day, guaranteed rate trend, or minimum balance recommendation. Record the current season, rate, and interface when the feature becomes playable. Until then, treat Gold Bars as a documented plan rather than an active farming method.

## What remains unconfirmed?

Exchange rates, fees, minimums, caps, reset dates, Gold Market inventory, cosmetic prices, regional availability, real-money purchases, reverse conversion, trading, Vault interaction, and version-1.0 carryover are 待确认. The official video and announcement describe direction, not a completed UI or final economy table.

The direct answer is that WARDOGS Gold Bars are planned as a persistent cosmetic-oriented currency created through Cash exchange and seasonal conversion. The system is not yet documented as a complete live marketplace, so every numerical or inventory claim needs later confirmation.
