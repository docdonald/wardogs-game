## What do towers do in WARDOGS?

Official material confirms that WARDOGS is built around three teams contesting a randomized Control Zone, with a smaller Hot Zone creating additional pressure and reward. Test footage and community reports connect battlefield towers and their terminals to that objective flow. The official sources do not publish a final tower count, code format, or universal interaction sequence.

The safest way to understand a tower is as an objective-side position, not as a separate win condition. Holding or approaching it matters when it helps your team move, defend, gather information, or influence the active zone. A tower that is far from the scoring pressure can become a distraction even if it looks strategically strong.

## How should a squad approach a tower terminal?

Treat the terminal as the last step of a controlled approach. Scout the area, identify defenders and vehicle sightlines, clear immediate threats, and place cover before one player interacts. The remaining players should watch entrances and the third faction’s route instead of stacking on the same prompt.

Read the current on-screen instruction rather than memorizing a test video. Keybinds and interaction behavior changed during testing, and the official record does not publish a permanent terminal key. If a prompt is missing or behaves unexpectedly, capture the build and report it instead of applying an unverified config edit.

## Are tower codes confirmed?

Community test reports describe collecting code fragments from controlled towers and entering a code at a terminal to affect Hot Zone movement. This is useful evidence of a build-sensitive mechanic, but it is not an official permanent rules table. The number of fragments, code length, order, expiry, and activation location remain “待确认.”

## How do towers relate to Control Zone and Hot Zone?

The Control Zone is the published scoring area where the team with the strongest presence earns points, while the Hot Zone is a smaller, higher-risk area that official material associates with double cash. Towers sit in the broader movement and interaction layer described by current test evidence. Their tactical value comes from helping a team connect positions to the active objective.

A tower capture does not replace holding the scoring area. Rotate infantry, vehicles, logistics, and medical support toward the current pressure after the interaction completes. The official sources do not confirm whether a tower itself scores points, how long an effect lasts, or whether every map uses the same arrangement.

## Why are three teams important at a tower?

A two-team fight can create a brief sense of safety after one side retreats. In WARDOGS, the third faction can use that moment to attack the terminal, the route, or the squad that just spent its resources. Protect the approach and keep information on the unused direction.

Do not put every player on the terminal. Leave a small reserve, identify a fallback, and coordinate a transport or supply response if the objective moves. Faction-specific tower bonuses, team ownership rules, and alliance behavior are “待确认.”

## How do FOBs and building support towers?

Team17 confirms player-built forward operating bases, fortifications, destruction, and logistics. A squad may use construction to protect a route near a useful objective position, but the official page does not say that every tower is a valid FOB location or that a structure is permanent. Build only when the current zone and supply route justify the spend.

Community observations mention drills and Hot Zone movement, yet those reports are version-sensitive. Do not assume a tower interaction consumes a fixed resource, lasts a fixed duration, or overrides all other zone behavior. The current build prompt and official update notes take priority.

## What are common tower mistakes?

The most common mistake is capturing or defending a tower without checking what it changes for the active objective. Another is camping the structure while the Hot Zone moves elsewhere. A third is copying an old code, timer, or keybind and treating it as a permanent rule.

Use the tower to solve a map problem, then rotate when the problem changes. Bring cover, information, and a route for the squad rather than relying on the terminal alone. Exact capture rewards, building costs, drill fuel, and destruction rules are “待确认.”
