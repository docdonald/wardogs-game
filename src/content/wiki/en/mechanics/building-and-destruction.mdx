## What is confirmed about WARDOGS building and destruction?

The official Steam and Team17 pages list building and destruction as core mechanics in the large-scale tactical sandbox. They give concrete examples: players can fortify strategic positions or a forward operating base, while rockets and heavily armored vehicles can damage buildings and push through towns. The sources confirm the direction of the system, not a complete construction or damage manual.

That boundary is important for search answers. The collected material does not confirm that every wall can collapse, that every building has the same durability, or that players can repair every damaged object. Those details are “待确认” until BULKHEAD publishes them or the live build exposes them.

## How does building help a Control Zone team?

Building is presented as a way to improve a tactical position, not as a separate base-building mode. A fortified chokepoint can protect a route, delay an enemy push, or give a squad a safer place to stage near the objective. The usefulness of a structure depends on the randomized Control Zone and the roads, buildings, bridges, or ridgelines that matter in that match.

Construction also interacts with logistics. A team may need a protected route for supplies or a position that lets transport reach the scoring area. The official sources do not publish material costs, placement limits, construction time, or a complete list of buildable objects, so players should treat any exact build order as version-specific.

## How do rockets and armored vehicles destroy cover?

Steam’s examples describe rocket launchers damaging apartment blocks and armored vehicles tearing through towns. The reveal material shows a battlefield where the environment reacts to player action, while the Team17 page frames destruction as part of tactical warfare. These examples support the conclusion that some urban cover and structures can be affected by heavy weapons.

They do not prove a universal destruction rule. The source pack does not provide damage thresholds, collapse chains, penetration values, repair behavior, or a list of buildings that can be removed. A player should therefore use destruction to create pressure and deny cover without promising that a particular wall will always fall.

## Is construction a counter to destruction?

The collected competitor reference describes building as the counterpoint to destruction: teams can create useful cover and fortify ground, while attackers can use explosives or armored vehicles to challenge it. That relationship fits the official descriptions of an objective sandbox where every decision affects the battlefield. It does not mean every defensive object has a direct destructive counter.

The best tactical question is what the position is protecting. A fortification that blocks a transport lane may be valuable even if it is temporary, while a structure that traps a squad can become a liability when the zone moves. Coordinate placement with the objective and keep a route open for retreat or reinforcement.

## What information is still missing?

Public sources do not give a full construction catalogue, material types, hit points, repair rules, collapse behavior, damage falloff, or a definitive statement about the percentage of the map that is destructible. They also do not publish a complete vehicle armor table or rocket damage table. These fields should be tracked as “待确认” rather than filled with values from another game.

Early Access can revise the system as the team gathers feedback. A trailer frame or a competitor page can show a possibility, but the live build and official notes are needed for a stable mechanic reference. Update this page when a dated source answers one of the missing questions.

## Beginner building checklist

Build for a team problem: protect a chokepoint, help a transport route, or hold a useful staging position. Do not spend resources on a structure that the randomized objective will immediately abandon. Watch how the enemy uses rockets and armored vehicles, then adapt the position instead of assuming any fortification is permanent.

The confirmed WARDOGS promise is a reactive battlefield where construction and destruction affect the same Control Zone fight. Exact objects, values, and repair behavior remain “待确认.”
