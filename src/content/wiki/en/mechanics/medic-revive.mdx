## Is there a Medic class in WARDOGS?

The official answer is that WARDOGS is classless in the traditional locked-role sense. The developer FAQ says players can purchase equipment and vehicles to define what they do for a life, and it uses Medic as an example of a possible progression direction. That means a player can prepare medical support without choosing a permanent class at account creation.

This distinction matters when reading community guides. A “Medic build” describes a loadout and a playstyle, not a guaranteed faction job or squad slot. The official sources do not confirm a mandatory Medic, a fixed team composition, or a permanent medical specialization.

## Does reviving teammates earn cash?

Yes. Team17 explicitly lists reviving squadmates among the teamplay actions that reward cash. A revive also returns a player to the fight, which can restore friendly presence near the Control Zone and prevent the cost of immediately buying another life. The exact cash amount, revive state, and reward timing are “待确认.”

Do not turn that statement into a payout guarantee. A test build may change the eligible distance, the required item, or the reward event. Use the current UI and official update notes to verify the live behavior before publishing an amount or a best-income ranking.

## What should a beginner do before attempting a revive?

Read the fight before moving toward the casualty. Ask whether the player is inside the objective, whether an enemy still has line of sight, and whether your squad can create cover. A quick revive that exposes two additional players can lose more cash and presence than it saves.

Communicate the approach, use available cover, and keep one teammate watching the likely enemy route. If the casualty is too exposed, delay the interaction until pressure moves or smoke/terrain creates a safer window. The public material does not publish a universal revive animation time, range, or interruption rule.

## Which medical items are confirmed?

The official FAQ confirms that players can purchase specialist items and names medical support as one possible role. Existing research also records bandages, medkits, and defibrillator-style tools in test observations, but it does not provide a final item table. Treat every specific item name, price, carry count, or unlock level outside the official page as “待确认.”

Choose items around the job you actually accepted. A squad moving through a contested route may need a compact medical option, while a dedicated support player may carry more recovery tools at the cost of other utility. The game’s free-form loadout system means there is no single medical package that is correct for every life.

## How does medical support help the Control Zone?

The Control Zone awards the team with the strongest presence under the published rules. A revive can put a friendly player back into that area, preserve a squad’s ability to contest, and reduce the time spent returning from base. This is why medical support has strategic value even when it produces few eliminations.

The Hot Zone can offer double cash but also concentrates danger. A medic should not enter the smaller high-risk area solely for the reward if the squad cannot cover the rescue. Exact Hot Zone revive bonuses, presence weighting, and medical multipliers are “待确认.”

## Can a medic earn money without fighting?

The official economy is designed to reward helpful actions, not only kills. Reviving teammates is a direct example, and the wider support loop includes transport, logistics, spotting, and objective control. A medical player can therefore contribute through survival and positioning while using a weapon only when necessary.

This does not make the role risk-free. Carrying expensive medical equipment, crossing open ground, or reviving under fire can still lose the entire loadout. Keep the purchase proportional to the route, stay near a squad, and review the balance after the match rather than relying on an unverified payout chart.

## How should squads organize revives?

One player should not be the only person watching casualties. Call the downed location, identify the threat direction, and decide who provides cover, who interacts, and who keeps the squad moving. A transport or logistics player can also help by bringing the team back to the objective after a failed push.

Because WARDOGS has three factions, a fight between two teams can invite the third. Do not stack every player around the revive prompt or assume the immediate shooter is the only threat. The official sources do not define formal squad medical roles, so the exact division remains a coordination choice.

## What are the biggest revive mistakes?

The most common mistake is treating the revive prompt as more important than the objective. A second mistake is buying medical gear without confirming that the squad will use it. A third is publishing a community test value as a permanent rule when the developer has not documented the item or timing.

Use the current build to validate controls, medical inventory, and the result of a successful rescue. Preserve screenshots or logs for a future update, and mark old values by date. Until then, use “待确认” for revive duration, radius, cooldown, XP, and exact reward.

## What remains unconfirmed?

The official record confirms medical equipment as a player choice and reviving squadmates as a rewarded team action. It does not confirm a locked Medic class, a final medical catalogue, item prices, revive range, animation duration, cooldown, downed-state rules, or Medic XP formula. Those fields should remain clearly unconfirmed.

The direct answer is to treat Medic as a flexible support loadout: communicate, create cover, revive when the rescue preserves objective presence, and keep the cost of the kit under control. The role is real in the teamplay loop, while its detailed numbers remain version-dependent.
