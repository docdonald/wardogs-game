## What is WARDOGS Proximity Voice Chat?

WARDOGS Proximity Voice Chat is local voice communication between nearby players. The official Steam and Team17 descriptions say it can be used to talk tactics with teammates and interact with enemy players, tying voice to the game’s player-driven battlefield. Squad communication is a separate coordination layer for an organized group.

That design makes information part of the fight. A nearby opponent may be able to hear or answer a player, while a squad can continue coordinating when members spread away from the local conversation. The public sources confirm the feature exists, but they do not publish a final voice radius, attenuation model, default push-to-talk key, or subtitle policy.

## How is local voice different from squad chat?

Local voice is tied to proximity; squad communication is tied to group membership. A player may use local speech to communicate with anyone nearby, including an enemy, while squad tools are intended to keep the dedicated group aligned across a wider battlefield. The exact channel selector and whether both channels can be heard at the same time are not listed in the collected sources.

This distinction matters for a large objective. A pilot, transport passenger, forward infantry element, and support player may need a common plan even after they are no longer physically close. At the same time, a player entering a building or crossing a route may encounter an opponent who is outside the squad but inside local voice range.

## What controls are publicly documented?

The collected competitor research, which cites the official Steam and developer material, records individual mute controls, a mute-all-VoIP option, and the ability to disable text chat. These controls are useful safety and focus tools for a three-team battle where local conversation may include opponents. They should be presented as the current documented controls rather than a complete final settings manual.

## Why does voice matter to the Control Zone?

The Control Zone rewards presence, support, and timing, so a short call can change whether a squad reaches the objective or loses a vehicle route. Local voice can warn nearby teammates, coordinate a revive, or expose that an enemy is close. Squad communication can carry the longer plan: who is transporting, who is building, who is watching a flank, and who is returning to the zone.

Voice is not a replacement for the scoreboard or map information. Players still need to read the active objective, faction positions, and available routes. The official material also says teamplay actions reward cash, so communication can support both the faction win condition and the player’s persistent balance without turning voice itself into a published reward event.

## How should players use it responsibly?

Use the squad channel for concise plans and local voice when the nearby situation requires it. If enemy speech creates distraction, mute the individual or disable VoIP rather than assuming every player has the same tolerance. The current material does not specify a final moderation workflow, so players should use the platform’s available reporting tools and follow the game’s published code of conduct when it becomes available.

Do not treat voice proximity as guaranteed intelligence. The game may change distance, occlusion, or channel behavior during testing, and a video can show a conversation without proving its exact range. Treat any measured distance as version-specific until an official settings reference or reproducible public build confirms it.
