## What is a FOB in WARDOGS?

FOB means forward operating base: a player-built position that can help a team operate closer to the active battlefield. Team17 lists building, fortifying strategic chokepoints, transporting supplies, and destroying structures as part of the WARDOGS sandbox. The official material supports the role of a FOB, but it does not publish a complete construction manual.

Treat a FOB as an answer to a team problem, not as a permanent home. A useful position can shorten a route, protect a supply line, or give a squad cover while the Control Zone changes. If the objective moves away or the approach becomes indefensible, the same structure can become an expensive distraction.

## Where should you build a FOB?

Choose a location that connects a useful route to the active objective. A chokepoint, protected approach, or supply position can be more valuable than the visually strongest building if the zone is moving away. Before spending resources, check where your squad will enter, where vehicles can approach, and which direction the other two factions are likely to use.

The public sources do not publish a fixed list of best FOB coordinates. Map variation, randomized Control Zone placement, destruction, and enemy pressure can change the value of a location between matches. Any “best spot” from a test session must be labeled with its build and map context.

## How does construction help the objective?

Construction creates cover, defensive layers, and a place to organize supplies around a route. It can keep friendly players in useful space for longer, which supports Control Zone presence and the team’s cash loop. A FOB is most valuable when it lets the squad return to the fight, protect a logistics path, or delay an enemy push.

Do not build without a purpose. A structure far outside the current objective can consume time while the opposing factions score. The official sources confirm the strategic idea, but they do not confirm a build menu, placement radius, completion reward, or structure health values.

## How do supplies connect to a FOB?

Team17 describes helicopters ferrying supplies to teammates and places FOBs inside the broader logistics system. Supplies therefore matter because they allow a forward position to continue supporting infantry, vehicles, and construction. A logistics player should confirm that the receiving group can use the delivery before committing a vehicle or cargo run.

Exact supply types, pallet costs, drop controls, capacity, and conversion steps are not listed in the collected sources. Do not copy a Beta payout or cargo recipe into a permanent database without a dated source. The safe page-level instruction is to coordinate destination, approach, and recovery, then verify the live build’s interaction prompt.

## How should a squad defend a FOB?

Defend the route and the people using the FOB rather than stacking every player on one wall. Assign observation toward the likely approach, keep a reserve for a flank, and make sure transport and medical support can still move. The third faction can exploit a fight between the other two, so a position that looks secure from one direction may be exposed from another.

Construction is not invulnerability. Team17 explicitly includes destruction, rocket launchers, armored vehicles, and towns that react to combat. The public record does not confirm the health of specific walls, repair rules, anti-vehicle emplacements, or a universal defense layout, so those details remain not listed in the collected sources.

## When should you leave or rebuild?

Leave when the active zone, supply route, or team presence no longer justifies the position. A FOB that is isolated from the objective can create the illusion of progress while the other teams gain scoring pressure. Rebuild only after identifying the new route and confirming that the squad has the resources to support it.

The best decision may be to use the existing structure as a temporary fallback while moving the main force. This preserves some cover without forcing every player to defend an outdated location. The game does not publish a fixed relocation cooldown, destruction refund, or rebuild penalty, so those systems are not listed in the collected sources.

## What are common FOB mistakes?

The first mistake is building for appearance instead of movement. The second is starting a long construction project without a supply plan. The third is assuming a FOB creates a guaranteed personal spawn or makes a location safe; neither claim is confirmed by the official sources collected here.

Another mistake is using a test video as a permanent rules sheet. Interface prompts, materials, durability, and the relationship between towers, FOBs, and Hot Zone movement can change between builds. Use current official announcements and the live interaction prompt whenever a detail affects spending or survival.

## What does the official record leave open?

Official sources confirm that WARDOGS combines base building, destruction, logistics, vehicles, and objective pressure. They do not confirm the exact construction catalog, resources, price, durability, demolition method, spawn behavior, repair system, or tower-drill rules. Every one of those fields needs a published source before use. the developer publishes them or a reproducible live build verifies them.

The direct answer is that a FOB is a forward team position: build it where it helps movement and objective presence, supply it deliberately, defend its approaches, and abandon it when the zone makes it irrelevant. The strategic principle is stable even while the numbers and UI remain version-dependent.
