## What is the WARDOGS Lonestar faction?

Lonestar is one of the three teams in WARDOGS. The official faction description calls it “the heavy-hitters in the Western paramilitary world,” which establishes its setting identity rather than a confirmed combat statistic. Lonestar fights Valkyra and Manticore in the same Control Zone match structure.

The phrase “heavy-hitters” should not be read as proof of a heavy-weapon, armour, health, or damage bonus. The public sources do not publish a Lonestar-only arsenal, vehicle pool, skill tree, economy rule, or fixed class. ## How does Lonestar fit the match objective?

All three factions contest the same randomized Control Zone. The team with the strongest player presence receives the objective point at the published scoring interval, and the first team to reach the stated victory target wins. The scoring rule belongs to the match, not to a special Lonestar version of the objective.

This makes Lonestar a side in a three-way battlefield rather than a separate mode.
