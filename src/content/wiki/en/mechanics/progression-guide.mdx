## How does WARDOGS progression work?

WARDOGS progression is not one single bar. The collected sources separate persistent cash, XP progression, and match or faction score into different systems. Cash pays for weapons, equipment, utility, and vehicles; XP develops role-based tracks; Control Zone points determine whether a team wins the match.

Keeping those systems separate prevents a common mistake: a large account balance is not proof of a high role level, and a winning team does not mean every player received identical XP. A revive may help the objective and earn cash, while its exact XP event remains unpublished. The current public record supports the structure, not a complete equation.

## What are the six XP tracks?

The developer material describes six role-based XP progression tracks. Medic and Pilot are confirmed track names in the collected reference, while the complete public list and all thresholds are not available in a verified official table. The safest page-level answer is therefore six announced routes with only the confirmed names stated explicitly.

XP is intended to reflect different ways of contributing, not only eliminations. Revives, piloting, logistics, combat, and other activities are discussed as role directions, but the exact event list, amount, and multiplier for each track are not listed in the collected sources. Do not convert a community label into an official track name without a source.

## How is XP different from persistent cash?

Cash starts at the published $10,000 balance, is earned by team-helpful actions, and persists from match to match. It immediately affects what a player can buy for the next life. XP is a longer-term progression record associated with role tracks, but the sources do not confirm that every XP level grants a specific item or passive combat benefit.

This distinction matters when choosing a loadout. An item may require progression access and still cost cash, or a future system may use XP for another reward; the public documents do not settle that relationship. Treat any claim about level-to-price conversion or permanent stat bonuses as not listed in the collected sources.

## What progression systems are planned for Early Access?

The Steam Early Access information describes deeper progression systems, a stronger seasonal metagame, and rewards for the time and impact players bring through combat, support, or logistics. The roadmap reference also lists Gold Bars, planned Player Skills, challenges, a Black Market, businesses, and a Vault. These are development plans and may be introduced or revised during Early Access.

The roadmap describes a planned seasonal structure in which cash and XP can reset while cash converts to Gold Bars, with Gold Bars, cosmetics, and planned Player Skills serving as longer-term progression. Those longer-term systems remain subject to change, but Season 1 now has a dated XP and unlock changelog for the live Early Access build.

## How should you progress efficiently?

Play the role that helps the active objective and observe which actions produce cash and XP in the current build. A medic-style support life, pilot transport run, logistics route, or direct combat life can all contribute to the wider team, but the source pack does not justify a universal fastest-XP method. The correct route may also change when seasonal systems or balance updates arrive.

Use persistent cash conservatively while learning. A high-cost vehicle or specialist setup may create value, but losing it can reduce the options available on a later life. Review both the immediate objective result and the longer-term progression signal after a match.

## Is there a confirmed 1.0 progression reset?

No final 1.0 reset schedule is confirmed in the official sources collected here. The roadmap reference describes a rough target of about 24 months from Early Access to version 1.0 and a conditional seasonal economy, but it explicitly treats the roadmap as a direction rather than a dated calendar. Treat exact reset dates, conversion values, and permanent reward guarantees as not listed in the collected sources.
