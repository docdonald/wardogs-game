## What does logistics mean in WARDOGS?

Logistics is the work of keeping a team supplied and mobile while it contests the Control Zone. Team17 specifically describes ferrying supplies by helicopter and transporting friendly players to the objective as valid ways to play and earn cash. The role connects the economy, vehicles, FOB construction, and the team’s ability to maintain presence.

This is not a separate mode with a published checklist or permanent class. WARDOGS lets players buy the tools they want for each life, so the same player can switch between transport, medical support, combat, or construction. Exact vehicle seats, cargo slots, fuel, and payout amounts are not listed in the collected sources.

## How do transport runs help a squad?

A transport run turns distance into objective presence. Move players toward the active Control Zone, deliver them where they can survive, and communicate the approach so the receiving group is ready. The official economy description says transporting friendlies to the zone rewards cash, which means a pilot or driver can contribute without leading the kill feed.

The destination matters more than a full vehicle meter. If the zone has shifted, a crowded landing point may be worse than a safer edge where the squad can regroup. The public material does not confirm whether partial trips, passengers who dismount early, or failed insertions receive the same reward, so those details remain not listed in the collected sources.

## How do supply runs support a FOB?

Supplies let a forward position continue building, defending, and supporting the team. Team17 places helicopter supply ferrying, FOB construction, and fortification in the same player-driven sandbox, so logistics should be planned around what the receiving position can use. Confirm the destination with the squad before committing a cargo vehicle.

Do not assume every visible crate has the same function. The official sources do not publish a final cargo catalog, pallet price, delivery control, or breakdown procedure. A community Beta guide may record a useful observation, but the value must be labeled with its version and never treated as a universal Early Access rule.

## How should you plan a safe route?

Read the active zone, identify likely enemy approaches, and choose a route with cover or room to turn back. Coordinate the drop timing with infantry, medics, and builders so the delivery produces immediate value. A transport that arrives after the fight has moved may earn less strategic value even if the vehicle survives.

Three teams make the route a live information problem. The faction that is not currently contesting your landing can still intercept your return or attack the receiving position. Keep communication short, report threats early, and preserve enough fuel or cash for the next task; exact fuel and repair systems are not listed in the collected sources.

## Does logistics earn cash?

Yes, the official Team17 page names transporting friendlies and ferrying supplies as cash-rewarding teamplay actions. The Steam announcement also describes support and logistics as activities tied to the player’s account balance. The sources do not publish a complete payout table, a guaranteed per-minute rate, or a single best logistics route.

Measure the run by both income and outcome. A delivery that keeps a squad in the Control Zone, enables a FOB, or saves a vehicle route may help the team even when its personal payout is not the largest. Exact amounts, stacking, and failure penalties are not listed in the collected sources.

## How does logistics interact with the Hot Zone?

The Hot Zone is presented as a higher-risk area that can earn double cash. Transporting players or supplies near it can increase the potential value of a run, but it also concentrates all three factions and reduces the margin for a slow landing. Use the live map and squad call rather than treating the Hot Zone as a guaranteed delivery point.

If the receiving team cannot hold the area, a safer Control Zone edge may create more lasting presence. Protect the cargo, announce the approach, and leave before the route becomes a trap. The public sources do not confirm Hot Zone drop radii, passenger thresholds, or vehicle-specific multipliers.

## What should a new logistics player buy?

Buy the smallest transport or utility setup that matches the team request. If the task is moving infantry, prioritize a vehicle you can operate; if it is supplying a FOB, confirm cargo compatibility; if the squad needs a recovery run, preserve cash for the return. WARDOGS does not publish a fixed logistics class or mandatory loadout.

Avoid spending on a vehicle you cannot explain. Practice the route in a low-risk situation, keep communication open, and switch to a cheaper support life after a costly loss. Vehicle models, seats, cargo slots, fuel, repair, and prices are not listed in the collected sources.

## What should a logistics guide not claim?

It should not promise that every pilot earns more than a combat player, that one cargo type is always profitable, or that a particular drop key works in every build. The official footage and announcements establish the role and its purpose, not every interface detail. Mark community measurements and test-only behaviors clearly.
