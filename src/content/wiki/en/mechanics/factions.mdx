## Which factions are in WARDOGS?

WARDOGS has three officially named factions: Lonestar, Valkyra, and Manticore. They are the three competing teams that contest the Control Zone in a match of up to 100 players. Public sources confirm their names and broad identities, but do not publish a stable list of faction-exclusive weapons, vehicles, passive bonuses, skill trees, or economy rules.

The distinction is important because a faction is not a player class. WARDOGS uses purchased loadouts and flexible roles, so a player can support a team through medical, transport, construction, reconnaissance, or combat equipment regardless of the faction name. Kolchia is the fictional battlefield setting, not a fourth playable faction.

## What is known about Lonestar?

Official identity material describes Lonestar as a heavy-hitter in the Western paramilitary world. That wording establishes a political and military flavour, not a confirmed heavy-weapon bonus. The public record does not provide a complete history, named leaders, home territory, fixed uniforms, or a unique arsenal for Lonestar.

Lonestar should therefore be documented as one of the three match sides. If an Early Access build later gives the faction a distinct vehicle pool, voice set, spawn rule, or visual marker, that change should be recorded with the exact version and official evidence. Promotional colour or equipment appearance alone is not enough to establish asymmetry.

## What is known about Valkyra?

Valkyra is officially described as seeking to return the Soviet People’s Republic to greatness. The description provides world-building context and helps distinguish the faction from its opponents. It does not confirm a Soviet-only weapon list, a different economy, a special vehicle, or an automatic role assignment.

The Valkyra page in the collected evidence treats the faction as one side of the same three-team ruleset. Its players still contest the Control Zone, use the published loadout system, and contribute to the shared objective. Any future asymmetry must be demonstrated by an official rule or reproducible build behavior rather than inferred from the faction’s lore.

## What is known about Manticore?

Manticore is described in the official identity material as the Kingdom of Persia and Tehran’s shadow army. As with the other factions, that is a setting and identity statement. There is no confirmed public rule that gives Manticore a stealth bonus, different cash rate, exclusive vehicle, or unique objective interaction.

The faction can be compared by its published identity, not by invented statistics. A source-led page should keep “officially described identity” separate from “confirmed gameplay distinction.” At present, the latter is simply that Manticore is one of the three teams fighting for the same objective.

## How do factions relate to Control Zone scoring?

The three factions enter the same match and compete for player presence in the randomized Control Zone. The team with the strongest presence earns the published objective points, with the first team to reach 100 winning. The scoring system belongs to the match structure; no source says that Lonestar, Valkyra, or Manticore uses a different point value.

This means faction strategy is shaped by the three-way battlefield. A push against one opponent can expose the team to the third, and a temporary alignment of interests does not create a formal alliance. Squad organization sits inside a faction, while the faction is the full side competing against the other two.

## Do factions have unique equipment or roles?

No faction-specific bonuses, arsenals, vehicle restrictions, or fixed skill trees are published in the current evidence. Official material instead emphasizes custom purchases each life and the freedom to choose how to play. A Lonestar player and a Manticore player may both choose a support-oriented loadout while remaining enemies on the scoreboard.

The same caution applies to economy. Cash rewards are connected to combat and teamplay actions such as revives, transport, spotting, and objective work, not to a named faction bonus. Exact payout differences by faction are “待确认.”

## What should a faction guide include?

A useful faction page can safely include the three names, official identity descriptions, their role as match teams, the shared Control Zone rules, and the distinction between factions, squads, classes, and Kolchia. It should avoid presenting visual motifs as mechanics and should link every future asymmetric rule to an announcement or versioned in-game observation.

For now, the direct answer is simple: WARDOGS has Lonestar, Valkyra, and Manticore, and all three contest the same three-team objective. Their lore is public; their gameplay differences are not. That evidence boundary is more reliable than a speculative faction tier list.
