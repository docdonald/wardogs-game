## Which WARDOGS trailer should you watch first?

Start with Team17’s [Reveal Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hVtmnaUCpuQ), published alongside the February 2026 announcement. Then watch the [Future Games Show Gameplay Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DX7PG1z6J0o) for a longer look at the advertised 100-player, three-team battlefield. Finish with the [Gunplay short](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/7Aq6QUUIJoM) if your question is specifically about weapon handling.

This order follows the official release sequence, not a claim that one video is a newer final build. Each upload is pre-release footage and can show features or visuals that change during Early Access. Use the Steam page and current announcements for live access, prices, and version information.

## What does the Reveal Trailer confirm?

Team17’s [official announcement](https://www.team17.com/news/wardogs-reveal-trailer) introduces WARDOGS as a Tactical All Out Warfare FPS developed with BULKHEAD and planned for Steam Early Access. It describes one hundred players split across three teams fighting over a Control Zone on a destructible battlefield where cash is earned and cash is central. The announcement also links the official Steam, Discord, and Playtesting signup destinations.

The trailer establishes the game’s advertised identity and broad loop. It does not provide a complete weapons list, map catalogue, faction bonus table, or final control scheme. Read it as a reveal of direction and setting, not a technical specification sheet.

## What is different about the Gameplay Trailer?

The June Future Games Show video offers a longer pre-alpha gameplay view. Its official description highlights 100 players, three rival teams, Control Zone fighting, cash, and surviving long enough to bank the result. The Steam announcement adds that the extended footage shows tactical firefights, proximity voice chat, building, and destruction.
