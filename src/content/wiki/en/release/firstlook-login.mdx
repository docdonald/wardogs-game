## What is the official WARDOGS FirstLook Login?

The official WARDOGS login page sends an existing account to its dashboard. It lists Discord, Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, and Email login paths, and it provides a Sign Up route for players who do not yet have an account. The page is the correct place to begin account access; a copied login URL or a third-party key page is not an equivalent source.

Logging in and being invited are different states. The page says that users are logging into their dashboard, but it does not say that any login method automatically grants a Playtest slot. A player may have a valid FirstLook account and still need an invitation, a Steam entitlement, or the exact instructions for the current test.

## How do you reach the dashboard?

Open the WARDOGS community domain and choose Log In rather than searching for an old dashboard link. Select the provider connected to your registration, or use the Email option if that is how the account was created. After authentication, read the dashboard status and any current test instructions instead of assuming that an older Alpha or Beta message still applies.

If you do not have an account, use the Sign Up link on the same official domain. The community signup page describes future Playtests and offers Discord and Steam registration buttons. It also suggests wishlisting WARDOGS on Steam and joining the Community Discord, while warning that slots are limited and that participation is intended to produce feedback and bug reports.

## Which login methods are listed?

The official page lists Discord, Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, and Email. That list describes available identity providers on the page, not a ranking of invitation chances. Choosing Steam does not prove that the Steam account owns WARDOGS, and choosing Discord does not prove that the Discord account has a verified role.

Use the same provider that was linked during signup whenever possible. If the dashboard appears empty after using a different provider, do not create multiple accounts or hand credentials to someone offering to “merge” them. First confirm which email, Steam account, or Discord identity was used, then return to the official login page and contact the official support or community channel if the issue remains.

## Does FirstLook Login grant a Playtest invite?

No. The community login page only says that a user can access the dashboard. The signup page says that future Playtest slots are limited, and the Steam store separates Request Access from the game’s purchase and Early Access listing. These sources support account access and registration, but they do not promise selection for every registrant.

Historical announcements show why the distinction matters. During the documented Closed Beta, the developer described a guaranteed route for pre-orders and a separate random route through native Steam Playtest signup. Those rules belonged to the dated event. A FirstLook login today cannot be presented as a guarantee for a future test unless a new official announcement says so.

## What should you check on the dashboard?

Check whether the dashboard names WARDOGS, identifies a current test, displays an invitation state, or provides a link that matches the email you received. Record the date and wording if the page says you are on a list or waiting for selection. The public sources do not publish a universal status vocabulary, so any missing field needs a source rather than a guess.

Do not post screenshots containing email addresses, account IDs, invitation links, or one-time tokens. A dashboard image can be useful evidence for your own support request, but it is not a reusable key. The community page specifically directs invited users to continue through the link sent to their email, which is safer than forwarding a private link to strangers.

## What if login fails?

Start with the official domain and confirm that the provider account is the same one used at signup. Check the invitation email, Steam library, and current Steam announcement before changing account settings. A blank dashboard can mean that there is no current test, that a different identity was used, or that an invitation has not been issued; the collected sources do not provide one universal diagnosis.

Avoid unofficial browser extensions, key sellers, password requests, and pages that ask for a Steam API key. If the provider redirects to an unfamiliar domain, close the page and return to the official community link from Team17 or Steam. Keep the error message and timestamp for support, while leaving the cause unresolved until an official response is available.

## How does FirstLook relate to Steam?

FirstLook is an account and playtesting platform; Steam is the store and entitlement system. The FirstLook site describes waitlists, key distribution, NDAs, beta branches, surveys, and Discord management as platform capabilities, while the WARDOGS Steam page controls the game listing and Playtest request. A WARDOGS page may use both systems without making them interchangeable.

When a test opens, follow the sequence named by the official notice. It may ask you to register on the community page, request access on Steam, wait for an email, or use a dashboard link. Do not combine steps from separate builds, because Closed Alpha, Closed Beta, future Playtests, and Early Access can have different rules.

## FirstLook Login checklist

Use the official WARDOGS login page, choose the account provider connected to signup, and read the dashboard status. If you are new, register through the official signup page and keep the email account available for an invitation. Confirm any Steam entitlement separately, and never treat a successful login as proof that the game will launch.

The evidence-backed answer is that FirstLook Login is an account access step, not an Alpha Key generator. The dashboard can carry test instructions, but the current status, selection rules, hardware survey requirement, NDA, and next invitation wave may change. Follow the newest official Steam or community announcement and mark anything not stated there as not listed in the collected sources.
