## Is the WARDOGS beta still available?

The latest official notice changed Closed Beta 02 into an Open Beta for a short window that ended on Sunday at 08:00 UTC. The first Closed Beta on August 21–23 and the second test on September 3–6 were time-limited events, not permanent access states. At the time of this check, players should treat both windows as finished and look for a newer Steam announcement before expecting another test.

The September 5 post titled “THE CLOSED BETA IS NOW AN OPEN BETA!” invited players to help test the servers and linked back to the Steam store. It did not publish a later beta date. The announcement therefore confirms the ended window, while future access remains unpublished until BULKHEAD posts a new schedule.

## What was Closed Beta 02?

On September 1, the developers announced Closed Beta 02 ahead of The FPS Games Show. Pre-ordering guaranteed access to the event, while additional Steam Playtest access was handed out in waves. The stated purpose was a genuine scale test covering server scaling and browsing, Twitch Drops, downloadable-content entitlements, local voice communication, and the login queue.

The same announcement scheduled the beta to begin after The FPS Games Show on September 3 at 18:00 UTC. The Steam store later described the guaranteed pre-order window as September 3 at 19:00 UTC through September 6 at 08:00 UTC, with wider playtest access granted in waves. Keep the announcement and store wording together because the schedule was tied to that specific test.

## How did Closed Beta access work?

The official Steam announcement said preorders granted guaranteed access to the Closed Beta. It also explained that external key distribution was capped after the Closed Alpha, so the original signup process could no longer provide keys in the same way. Players in the Steam Playtest pool could still be selected randomly, but signup was never described as a guaranteed invitation.

The announcement also said the team was using the test to understand server scaling and stability before Early Access. A later schedule notice reported that the first wave of guaranteed invitations had been sent and that players could preload “WARDOGS Playtest” if Steam showed access. These details describe the test operation, not a current entitlement.

## What happened to the NDA?

The preorder announcement said the NDA for the Closed Alpha footage was lifted, allowing creators to publish banked footage. It separately warned that ETP Playtests would remain under NDA and told players to read Discord announcements carefully. This is why clips from one test should not automatically be treated as permission for every future test.

For any new playtest, follow the current rules in the invitation and official Discord notice. A community post or a creator video can show that a test existed, but it cannot override an NDA or establish access for an account that was not invited.

## Are future WARDOGS beta dates confirmed?

No beta date after the September 2026 window is confirmed in the materials reviewed here. The official community page remains a signup route for future playtesting and tells invited players to continue through an email link. It does not promise that every registrant receives an invitation or that another test will begin on a particular date.

Steam’s store page can show a Playtest request option when a test is open. Check that listing, the official Steam announcements, and the WARDOGS community signup page together. If the sources disagree, mark the status as not published until BULKHEAD publishes a dated update.

## Beta access checklist

Use the official Steam page to request a Playtest when the request option is present. If you receive an invitation, use the Steam entitlement or the email link specified by the official community dashboard rather than a third-party key seller. Do not pay someone for a beta key based only on a screenshot, because the collected sources provide no verification for unofficial offers.

The reliable historical facts are the August 21–23 Closed Beta, Closed Beta 02 and its September 3–6 access window, the September 5 Open Beta notice, and the separate Steam Playtest invitation route. Any new date, invitation wave, or access rule needs a new official source before it can be added to this page.
