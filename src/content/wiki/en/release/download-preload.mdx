## Where can you download WARDOGS?

Download WARDOGS from the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1867240/WARDOGS/). Early Access is live, so players with a purchase can install the Windows PC product from their Steam library. The September 3–6 beta window has ended, and any “WARDOGS Playtest” entry is a separate historical test client. Do not use third-party “preload,” key, or modified-client websites, because none of those links are part of the official access flow.

The Steam listing separates the main WARDOGS product from the temporary Playtest application. That distinction matters when a search result or an old screenshot says “WARDOGS Playtest.” A Playtest entitlement is tied to the Steam account and the test window; it is not a permanent replacement for the Early Access product.

## How did the Closed Beta preload work?

The official [Closed Beta Schedule](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/announcements/) said that the first wave of invitations had been sent and that eligible players could preload “WARDOGS Playtest” in Steam. The later Closed Beta 02 announcement used the same Steam Playtest model, with access guaranteed for pre-orders and additional invitations released in waves. Players needed to check the Steam library for access rather than downloading a file from the community signup site. These announcements framed the preload as preparation for a limited test, not as a public release.

The announcement also explained that guaranteed access was connected to pre-orders and that the team would manage the number of players to evaluate server scaling and stability. This is why a user could see the game page but still not see a downloadable Playtest client. Access, invitation state, and the active test dates all had to line up.

## Is the Playtest download the Early Access download?

No. The official [Beta Recap & Next Steps](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/allnews/) marked the first Closed Beta as over, and the September 5 Open Beta notice set the later test to end Sunday at 08:00 UTC. A Playtest client is a test build with a test entitlement, while the Early Access product is the store application scheduled for its own release stage. Files from one stage should not be copied into the other or presented as a shortcut.

The Steam store page describes Early Access as an incomplete product that can change as development continues. The official Steam announcements recorded the launch preload on September 9 and the Early Access release on September 10; the client can continue receiving updates after installation. Keep Steam’s automatic update behavior enabled and let the client validate its own files.

## What is the official preload process?

Open the Steam store page, sign in to the account that owns the game, and install the WARDOGS entry from your Steam library. If Steam shows an update or download button, allow the client to finish its own download and verification. If the entry is missing, check the purchase entitlement and the latest announcement before troubleshooting local files.

Do not interpret a search snippet, an old Discord message, or a creator video as a current preload promise. The official community signup page only registers interest for future tests; it does not provide a direct installer. A new test may use a different app ID, date, or invite process, so the current Steam announcement takes priority.

## Can you preload WARDOGS without an invite?

The Closed Beta announcement did not make the Playtest client a free public download. It described first-wave invites, pre-order access, and a controlled rollout intended to protect server stability. Those historical rules do not replace the current Early Access purchase requirement; a player without ownership should not expect the live product to appear as a free download.

Future access rules are version-dependent. The official [WARDOGS Playtesting Signup](https://community.wardogs.com/signup/community) is the correct place to register interest, but registration is not a guarantee of entry. The Early Access purchase and launch availability should be checked on Steam rather than inferred from a historical beta guide.

## How much storage does the download need?

The current Steam listing requires 50 GB of available storage for WARDOGS. This is the published requirement for the live product, not a guarantee of the final installed size or future patch space. A test client or community screenshot can show a different number because it may use another build.

The same caution applies to download bandwidth and unpacking space. Steam may require temporary space while it applies an update, and the final requirement can differ from the installed size. Check the Steam download panel immediately before installing and leave additional free space for patching.

## What should you do if the Playtest entry is missing?

First confirm that Steam is signed into the account used for the invitation or purchase. Then open the official store page and the latest announcement to verify that the relevant test window is open. If the test has ended, a missing or non-launching Playtest entry is expected and cannot be fixed by reinstalling an unofficial copy.

If the entitlement is active but the library entry is still missing, restart Steam, check downloads, and use Steam’s own support flow. Keep screenshots of the account state and the announcement date when contacting support. The collected sources do not publish a WARDOGS-specific command, registry edit, or third-party launcher, so such instructions are not listed in the collected sources.
