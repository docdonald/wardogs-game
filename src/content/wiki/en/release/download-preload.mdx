## Where can you download WARDOGS?

Download WARDOGS from the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1867240/WARDOGS/). The store page is the authoritative place to purchase the Windows PC product, request Playtest access when it is available, and receive the correct Steam depot. Do not use third-party “preload,” key, or modified-client websites, because none of those links are part of the official access flow.

The Steam listing separates the main WARDOGS product from the temporary Playtest application. That distinction matters when a search result or an old screenshot says “WARDOGS Playtest.” A Playtest entitlement is tied to the Steam account and the test window; it is not a permanent replacement for the Early Access product.

## How did the Closed Beta preload work?

The official [Closed Beta Schedule](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/announcements/) said that the first wave of invitations had been sent and that eligible players could preload “WARDOGS Playtest” in Steam. Players needed to check the Steam library for access rather than downloading a file from the community signup site. The same announcement framed the preload as preparation for a limited test, not as a public release.

The announcement also explained that guaranteed access was connected to pre-orders and that the team would manage the number of players to evaluate server scaling and stability. This is why a user could see the game page but still not see a downloadable Playtest client. Access, invitation state, and the active test dates all had to line up.

## Is the Playtest download the Early Access download?

No. The official [Beta Recap & Next Steps](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/allnews/) marked the Closed Beta as over and directed players to future announcements. A Playtest client is a test build with a test entitlement, while the Early Access product is the store application scheduled for its own release stage. Files from one stage should not be copied into the other or presented as a shortcut.

The Steam store page describes Early Access as an incomplete product that can change as development continues. That means a preload package, if offered before release, may be updated before launch and may not contain the same build that testers used. Keep Steam’s automatic update behavior enabled and let the client validate its own files.

## What is the official preload process?

Open the Steam store page, sign in to the account that owns the game or has a current Playtest entitlement, and use the Steam library entry that appears. If Steam shows a pre-load button, start it from the library and allow the client to finish its own download and verification. If the entry is missing, check the active announcement and entitlement status before troubleshooting local files.

Do not interpret a search snippet, an old Discord message, or a creator video as a current preload promise. The official community signup page only registers interest for future tests; it does not provide a direct installer. A new test may use a different app ID, date, or invite process, so the current Steam announcement takes priority.

## Can you preload WARDOGS without an invite?

The Closed Beta announcement did not make the Playtest client a free public download. It described first-wave invites, pre-order access, and a controlled rollout intended to protect server stability. Therefore, a player without an entitlement should not expect the Playtest download to appear simply because the store page is visible.

Future access rules are version-dependent. The official [WARDOGS Playtesting Signup](https://community.wardogs.com/signup/community) is the correct place to register interest, but registration is not a guarantee of entry. The Early Access purchase and launch availability should be checked on Steam rather than inferred from a historical beta guide.

## How much storage does the download need?

The official sources collected for this page do not provide a stable Playtest or Early Access install-size figure. A file size observed during a test can change when the developer replaces assets, adds maps, or packages a new build. Treat any exact storage number from a community post as “待确认” until Steam displays it for the current product.

The same caution applies to download bandwidth and unpacking space. Steam may require temporary space while it applies an update, and the final requirement can differ from the installed size. Check the Steam download panel immediately before installing and leave additional free space for patching.

## What should you do if the Playtest entry is missing?

First confirm that Steam is signed into the account used for the invitation or purchase. Then open the official store page and the latest announcement to verify that the relevant test window is open. If the test has ended, a missing or non-launching Playtest entry is expected and cannot be fixed by reinstalling an unofficial copy.

If the entitlement is active but the library entry is still missing, restart Steam, check downloads, and use Steam’s own support flow. Keep screenshots of the account state and the announcement date when contacting support. The collected sources do not publish a WARDOGS-specific command, registry edit, or third-party launcher, so such instructions are “待确认.”

## What remains unconfirmed?

The official record confirms the Steam download path and the historical Playtest preload. It does not confirm a permanent Playtest download, a final Early Access preload start time, the exact install size, preload encryption rules, or early unlock timing. Those details should be added only after a dated Steam or Team17 announcement publishes them.

The practical answer is simple: use Steam, distinguish WARDOGS Playtest from WARDOGS Early Access, and follow the current announcement for entitlement and timing. A page that preserves that distinction will stay useful after a test closes instead of sending players to an obsolete installer.
