## How do you join the WARDOGS playtest?

Use the official WARDOGS community signup page to register interest in future playtests. The page provides a Register option and a login route for people who already signed up. When a test is open on Steam, the official store page can also show a native Playtest request option.

Neither route is described as an automatic invitation. The community page says that invited players should use the link sent to their email and then log in to the dashboard. Steam’s request system is a separate access path, so a signup confirmation should not be treated as proof that your account can launch the test.

## What does the official sign-up page ask you to do?

The community page tells players who want future WARDOGS Playtests to wishlist the game on Steam and join the Community Discord. It frames those actions as ways to maximize the chance of a Pre-Alpha invite, not as a purchase requirement or a guaranteed selection rule. The same page warns that slots are extremely limited while the team scales testing.

The page also explains why feedback and bug reports matter. It says the test is not a typical AAA marketing “Beta” and asks participants to get involved with the development process. That language describes the purpose of the signup, but it does not publish a selection formula, a fixed invitation count, or a universal waiting time.

## What happens after signup?

The community signup flow collects your interest and provides a dashboard for invited users. If you receive an email invitation, follow the link from that message rather than searching for a copied URL. The collected source does not publish a guaranteed waiting time, selection rule, or fixed number of invitations.

The Steam route may show access directly in the library when your account is selected. During the documented Closed Beta, the developers said a small number of players could enter randomly through Steam Playtest signup, while preorder access was guaranteed for that specific beta window. That historical rule should not be assumed for a future test unless a new announcement repeats it.

## How should you check an invitation?

Start with the email account used for the signup and confirm that the message links back to the official community or Steam flow. Then open the FirstLook dashboard through the official login page and compare any status wording with the current Steam entitlement. A screenshot, reposted message, or creator video can provide context, but none of those alone proves that your account was selected.

The collected sources do not publish a permanent invite URL, a reusable token, or a public list of accepted players. Do not paste a copied key into an unrelated site and do not assume that another person’s access grants access to your account. If the dashboard and Steam library disagree, record the date and ask through the official support or announcement channel; the correct resolution is unavailable until the publisher responds.

## How do you tell a real invitation from a claim?

Check that the link points to the official WARDOGS community domain, Steam, or a current announcement from the official Steam Community page. A creator video can demonstrate what a test looked like, but it cannot prove that every viewer has access. Do not use an unofficial key seller or a screenshot as the only evidence of eligibility.

The official Team17 reveal announcement also links to future playtesting signup, the Steam page, and the WARDOGS Discord. Those links are useful starting points because they connect the same official identity across services. If a message asks for information beyond the official signup or Steam process, its legitimacy is not listed in the collected sources.

## What should you do if the test does not launch?

First confirm that the test is currently scheduled and that the correct Steam account is logged in. Then check the Steam store entitlement, the invitation email, and the official Discord or announcement channel for a maintenance notice. The collected sources do not provide a universal fix for every launch error, so a missing entitlement should not be “solved” by downloading an unofficial build.

If the page shows that registration is open but your dashboard has no invitation, wait for an official selection message. Signup alone is not evidence that a player was chosen. For a dated test, compare the current schedule with the invitation terms and mark any conflicting information for follow-up until the developers clarify it.

## How are playtests, Alpha, Beta, and Early Access different?

The source set distinguishes the original Closed Alpha, later Closed Beta, Steam Playtest requests, and the planned Early Access release. The Alpha used a limited external-key process; the Closed Beta announcement described random Steam Playtest selection and a separate preorder guarantee for that event. Early Access is a commercial launch path and should not be treated as an invitation to a closed test.

These labels matter when reading older videos and forum posts. A creator may be describing an Alpha build while a Steam announcement is describing a Beta weekend, and the same menu or rule may not exist in a later build. Keep the test name, event dates, and source URL together whenever you update this page.

## Playtest access checklist

Register at the official community page, request access on Steam when the option is visible, and watch the email account linked to your signup. Use the exact Steam or dashboard instructions included with an invitation. Keep the Closed Beta, future Playtest, and Early Access purchase separate, because each can have different eligibility and timing.

The confirmed process is simple: official signup creates interest, Steam may provide a request or entitlement, and an invitation supplies the final instructions. No source reviewed here guarantees access for every registrant or publishes a permanent playtest date.

## What should you do while waiting?

Wishlist WARDOGS on Steam, join the Community Discord through the publisher’s link, and watch the official Steam announcement archive. The archive is the best place to distinguish a current test notice from a finished event, while Discord can carry live reminders under its current server rules. Keep your account email available and avoid third-party sellers claiming to have spare Alpha Keys.

If no new test is open, there is no action that can manufacture access. The reliable next step is to recheck the official signup, login dashboard, Steam page, and dated announcement when the team opens another window. Any new date, eligibility rule, or key policy remains unpublished until a current official post supports it.
