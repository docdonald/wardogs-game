## How do you join the WARDOGS playtest?

Use the official WARDOGS community signup page to register interest in future playtests. The page provides a Register option and a login route for people who already signed up. When a test is open on Steam, the official store page can also show a native Playtest request option.

Neither route is described as an automatic invitation. The community page says that invited players should use the link sent to their email and then log in to the dashboard. Steam’s request system is a separate access path, so a signup confirmation should not be treated as proof that your account can launch the test.

## What happens after signup?

The community signup flow collects your interest and provides a dashboard for invited users. If you receive an email invitation, follow the link from that message rather than searching for a copied URL. The collected source does not publish a guaranteed waiting time, selection rule, or fixed number of invitations.

The Steam route may show access directly in the library when your account is selected. During the documented Closed Beta, the developers said a small number of players could enter randomly through Steam Playtest signup, while preorder access was guaranteed for that specific beta window. That historical rule should not be assumed for a future test unless a new announcement repeats it.

## How do you tell a real invitation from a claim?

Check that the link points to the official WARDOGS community domain, Steam, or a current announcement from the official Steam Community page. A creator video can demonstrate what a test looked like, but it cannot prove that every viewer has access. Do not use an unofficial key seller or a screenshot as the only evidence of eligibility.

The official Team17 reveal announcement also links to future playtesting signup, the Steam page, and the WARDOGS Discord. Those links are useful starting points because they connect the same official identity across services. If a message asks for information beyond the official signup or Steam process, its legitimacy is “待确认.”

## What should you do if the test does not launch?

First confirm that the test is currently scheduled and that the correct Steam account is logged in. Then check the Steam store entitlement, the invitation email, and the official Discord or announcement channel for a maintenance notice. The collected sources do not provide a universal fix for every launch error, so a missing entitlement should not be “solved” by downloading an unofficial build.

If the page shows that registration is open but your dashboard has no invitation, wait for an official selection message. Signup alone is not evidence that a player was chosen. For a dated test, compare the current schedule with the invitation terms and mark any conflicting information as “待确认” until the developers clarify it.

## Playtest access checklist

Register at the official community page, request access on Steam when the option is visible, and watch the email account linked to your signup. Use the exact Steam or dashboard instructions included with an invitation. Keep the Closed Beta, future Playtest, and Early Access purchase separate, because each can have different eligibility and timing.

The confirmed process is simple: official signup creates interest, Steam may provide a request or entitlement, and an invitation supplies the final instructions. No source reviewed here guarantees access for every registrant or publishes a permanent playtest date.
