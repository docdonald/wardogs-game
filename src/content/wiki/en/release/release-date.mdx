## When does WARDOGS release?

WARDOGS entered Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. The official Steam listing now shows the product as Released and gives the same date for Early Access release. This is the playable public launch, not a confirmed date for the finished 1.0 release.

## What the September date represents

The September 10 date marks the beginning of the public Early Access period. Steam describes the product as fully playable but still a work in progress that may change. Players can expect the core experience while maps, weapons, vehicles, progression systems, balance, performance, and stability continue to evolve.

The full-release date is not confirmed in the collected sources. The Early Access FAQ says the team expects the period to last around one to two years, but it also says the exact duration can change with development and community feedback. Treat any exact 1.0 date as provisional until BULKHEAD or Team17 publishes one.

## How did access work before launch?

Before September 10, access depended on the test window and the route used to enter it. Closed Beta 02 ran September 3–6, with guaranteed access for pre-orders and additional Steam Playtest invitations released in waves; the final Open Beta notice ended that window Sunday at 08:00 UTC. Those test entitlements are historical and separate from the Early Access product.

The official community signup page is for future playtesting and does not promise that every signup receives an invitation. If you receive an invite, the community page instructs you to use the link sent by email and then log in to the dashboard. Always verify the invitation route through Steam, the official community page, or an official announcement.

## How to check for date changes

Start with the WARDOGS Steam store page because it carries the current release field and Early Access label. Then review the official Steam Community announcements, where the developers publish schedule and access notices. Team17’s game page and reveal announcement are useful historical references, but they may retain the broader “2026” wording after Steam has a more precise date.

External coverage can provide context about a test weekend or preorder access, but it is not the authority for a changed launch date. The PC Gamer report collected for this page also described September 10 as the Early Access date and connected preorder access with the August Closed Beta. Use that report as a cross-check only, and return to Steam for the final status.

## Release-date checklist for players

If you are planning to play, check whether your account owns the Early Access product and whether Steam has a client update ready. Keep the distinction between a Playtest request, a Closed Beta invitation, and Early Access ownership clear. The game’s price, access terms, and build can change during development, so a page or video published before launch may be outdated.

The confirmed information collected here is the September 10, 2026 Steam Early Access release and the existence of earlier test routes. A final 1.0 release date, exact launch time zone, and any future delay are not confirmed in the sources reviewed for this page.
