## When does WARDOGS release?

WARDOGS is scheduled to launch into Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. The date appears on the official Steam store listing and is repeated in the developer’s Steam announcement. It should be read as the date the Early Access build becomes available, not as a confirmed date for the finished 1.0 release.

The earlier Team17 reveal announcement only promised Steam Early Access during 2026. The later Steam listing provides the more specific date, so it is the better reference for the current schedule. If Steam changes the store date or an official announcement supersedes it, this page should be updated rather than relying on older articles.

## What the September date represents

The September 10 date describes the beginning of the public Early Access period. Steam still labels the product as Early Access, and the store explains that the game is a work in progress that may change. That means players can expect a playable core experience while maps, weapons, vehicles, progression systems, balance, performance, and stability continue to evolve.

The full-release date is not confirmed in the collected sources. The Early Access FAQ says the team expects the period to last around one to two years, but it also says the exact duration can change with development and community feedback. Treat any exact 1.0 date as provisional until BULKHEAD or Team17 publishes one.

## Can you play before the release date?

Before September 10, access depends on the test window and the route used to enter it. The Steam page provides a Playtest request option, while the preorder announcement describes guaranteed Closed Beta access for eligible preorders during the August test window. Those are separate from the Early Access launch itself and should not be presented as permanent access.

The official community signup page is for future playtesting and does not promise that every signup receives an invitation. If you receive an invite, the community page instructs you to use the link sent by email and then log in to the dashboard. Always verify the invitation route through Steam, the official community page, or an official announcement.

## How to check for date changes

Start with the WARDOGS Steam store page because it carries the current release field and Early Access label. Then review the official Steam Community announcements, where the developers publish schedule and access notices. Team17’s game page and reveal announcement are useful historical references, but they may retain the broader “2026” wording after Steam has a more precise date.

External coverage can provide context about a test weekend or preorder access, but it is not the authority for a changed launch date. The PC Gamer report collected for this page also described September 10 as the Early Access date and connected preorder access with the August Closed Beta. Use that report as a cross-check only, and return to Steam for the final status.

## Release-date checklist for players

If you are planning to play, check whether the store currently says Early Access and whether your account has the required purchase or test entitlement. Keep the distinction between a Playtest request, a Closed Beta invitation, and Early Access ownership clear. The game’s release state, price, and access terms can change during development, so a page or video published before launch may be outdated.

The confirmed information collected here is the September 10, 2026 Steam Early Access date and the existence of earlier test routes. A final 1.0 release date, exact launch time zone, and any future delay are not confirmed in the sources reviewed for this page.
