## What vehicles are confirmed in WARDOGS?

The collected official sources confirm three vehicle roles rather than a complete named roster. Helicopters can ferry supplies to teammates, artillery tanks can rain destruction on enemy positions, and heavily armoured tanks can push through towns while the environment reacts. The sources do not publish a verified list of individual vehicle names, prices, crew seats, or performance values.

That means this WARDOGS vehicles list is a confirmed-role guide, not a fabricated database. Vehicles are bought as part of the custom loadout for each life, alongside weapons, gear, and utility. Exact vehicle availability may change as Early Access development and future content expansion continue.

## How do vehicles fit into each life?

Team17 and Steam describe a persistent cash system in which every player starts with $10,000 and buys a loadout for each life. Vehicles compete with weapons and equipment for that spending decision, so a vehicle purchase is part of the match economy rather than a free selection detached from the player’s resources. Cash persists from match to match, making the timing of a vehicle purchase part of the wider strategy.

The official examples connect vehicles to teamplay. Transporting friendlies or supplies supports the objective, while a vehicle chosen at the right time can turn the tide of a fight. The exact price of any vehicle, its replacement cost, and whether a destroyed vehicle affects later purchases are not listed in the collected sources.

## What does the helicopter do?

The helicopter’s confirmed role is transport. The official game page says players can ferry supplies to teammates by helicopter, placing the aircraft inside the logistics and support side of the sandbox. That wording supports a supply-transport use, but it does not confirm a complete passenger system, weapon loadout, or flight model.

Helicopter controls, takeoff and landing behavior, fuel, carrying limits, seats, damage, repair, and respawn rules are not listed in the collected sources. A future helicopter page can add those details after an official control guide or reliable in-game testing becomes available. Until then, claims about the best altitude, route, or landing key are not listed in the collected sources.

## What does an artillery tank do?

The official pages use an artillery tank as the example of a destructive, fire-support choice. The description says a player can man an artillery tank and rain down destruction on the enemy, which supports a role built around pressure from a distance. It does not provide a named model, range, shell type, reload time, crew requirement, or damage table.

The artillery role should therefore be explained through its confirmed relationship with the objective. It is one possible way to influence a strategic position while other teammates fight, transport, or fortify ground. Exact targeting interfaces, spotting requirements, ammunition costs, and friendly-fire rules remain not listed in the collected sources.

## What do heavily armoured tanks do?

Team17 and Steam describe heavily armoured tanks as vehicles that can tear through towns. The same official text says the world reacts to the player’s presence, linking heavy armour to the game’s destruction system and to changing cover. This confirms a breakthrough and pressure role, not a specific tank roster or guaranteed result against every building.

The sources do not say which structures are always destructible or how armour interacts with rockets and other weapons. They also do not publish health, speed, weak points, repair, crew positions, or ammunition. Any tank comparison using exact survivability or damage numbers should remain unpublished until a verified source exists.

## Are fighter jets part of the current vehicle list?

Steam’s Early Access notes say the developers plan to add more vehicle types, including fighter jets. That is a future plan, not confirmation that fighter jets are available in the current build or that their controls and combat systems are finished. The vehicles list should separate planned additions from the roles already described in the public game pages.

The same Early Access material warns that balance, features, and polish will continue to change. New vehicle types, map changes, and gameplay variety may expand the roster later. Release timing, aircraft names, costs, and availability are not listed in the collected sources.
