## Where can you find WARDOGS Official News?

The most reliable WARDOGS Official News sources are the Steam announcement archive, Team17’s news and game pages, and the official Team17 YouTube channel. Steam is the best chronological record for playtests, preorder notices, Beta recaps, development updates, and launch information. Team17 and YouTube provide the publisher’s product copy, trailers, and showcase material that should be linked back to the relevant date.

This page is an index and verification guide, not a replacement for the original posts. ## February 2026: the public reveal

Team17’s Reveal Trailer introduced WARDOGS as a tactical all-out-warfare FPS built around up to 100 players split across three teams. The official description highlighted the Control Zone, destructible battlefield, cash economy, and future playtest routes. This is the first major public announcement in the current timeline and the source for the game’s initial positioning.

The reveal video is useful for what the publisher showed and described, but it is not a complete technical specification. Do not use a trailer to fill in weapon damage, map boundaries, voice range, or final release content. Those fields belong to the Steam listing, developer FAQ, or later build-specific announcements.

## August testing announcements

The Steam archive contains separate posts for early testing and the August 21–23 Closed Beta. These announcements explain access routes, invitations, preload timing, server scaling, stability work, and the NDA status for that event. Because the tests are different builds, a detail observed in an Alpha should not automatically be copied into a Beta or Early Access guide.

The Closed Beta schedule and later recap are now historical records. They remain useful for the development timeline and for explaining why community screenshots or map names may be test-specific. Current access should be checked on the live Steam page rather than through an expired Beta promise.

## September 1, 2026: Closed Beta 02

The Steam announcement “WARDOGS | Closed Beta 02 Announcement” introduced a second beta before The FPS Games Show. The stated test areas included server scaling and browsing, Twitch Drops, DLC entitlements, VOIP, and the login queue.

The announcement scheduled the event to begin after The FPS Games Show on September 3 at 18:00 UTC. This was a dated test notice, not a permanent Playtest entitlement. After the window closes, players should use the current Steam listing and a newer announcement rather than an old invite message.

This notice is important for platform and system-requirement pages. ## How should a news archive handle videos?

Use official videos for release announcements, gameplay context, and visible features, but label the evidence type. The Reveal Trailer and Gameplay Trailer can support the game’s high-level three-team, Control Zone presentation; they cannot independently confirm a final map roster or numeric balance. The local materials include subtitle files for some videos, but where a subtitle track is missing or noisy, quote only a verified short phrase and link the video.

Readers should be able to distinguish an official statement, a visible gameplay observation, and a community interpretation. Keep those labels in the news entry and include the video publication date when available. Never turn a reaction video or competitor summary into official news.

A finished Closed Beta remains important history but is not an active access route; a roadmap target remains planned until a patch confirms it; and a trailer remains evidence of what was shown, not a guarantee of launch content.
