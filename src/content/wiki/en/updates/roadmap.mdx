## What does the WARDOGS Roadmap confirm?

The WARDOGS Roadmap is a development direction, not a fixed delivery calendar. Official Steam and Team17 material describes plans for more maps, weapons, vehicles, gameplay variety, progression depth, and objective variations during Early Access. The current planning horizon is roughly one to two years before version 1.0, but no fixed 1.0 date is published.

This distinction is the most important roadmap answer. A feature can be planned without being in the Early Access launch build, and a target can move after feedback or stability work. Every item on a wiki roadmap should therefore carry a status such as confirmed at launch, planned, shown in testing, or an open question, with a dated source.

## What content is planned to grow?

The published plans include additional maps, weapons, vehicles, gameplay variety, deeper progression, and more objective variations. The map plan currently describes three Early Access maps with three variations each, but the official material does not provide the complete names, rotation order, or the exact systems changed by every variation.

Future vehicles, including fighter jets in the roadmap discussion, are examples of planned expansion rather than launch guarantees. The same caution applies to named weapons, map locations, and mode variants. Do not turn a concept image or a developer example into an available item page until the build or an official patch note confirms it.

## What progression and economy changes are planned?

The roadmap evidence describes a broader metagame around Persistent Cash and XP. It includes seasonal Cash and XP resets, automatic Cash conversion into Gold Bars, Player Skills, challenges, a Black Market, businesses, a Vault, and cosmetic progression through a planned Gold Market. The concepts are public, but exact seasons, rates, prices, interfaces, and unlock requirements are not.

Cash is still used for each-life loadouts, weapons, equipment, utility, and vehicles. Gold Bars are described as a longer-term cosmetic currency, not a replacement for Control Zone points. The page should keep the immediate battlefield economy separate from planned account progression and avoid publishing a conversion table until the developer releases one.

## Is there a dated feature schedule?

No complete dated feature schedule is present in the collected official sources. Steam announcements provide dated checkpoints—such as the Beta recap, playtest schedule, preorder notice, and development updates—but they do not form a permanent monthly roadmap. The developer has emphasized feedback, balance, server stability, and continued polish, all of which can change sequencing.

A useful update page should link each roadmap change to the announcement that introduced or revised it. If a future post gives a milestone date, record the date, scope, platform, and whether it is a target or a commitment. Do not infer a quarter or release month from the order in a marketing video.

## How should players read the roadmap?

Treat the Early Access launch as the baseline and every later feature as conditional until it is shipped. At launch, use the Steam page to verify the playable core; after each patch, compare the actual build with the roadmap categories. A feature can also arrive in a different form from its early description, so wording should preserve the developer’s uncertainty.

Players looking for a specific item should check the current announcement archive rather than a static promise. For example, a planned map, fighter jet, Black Market, or challenge system needs a release note before it can be called available. This approach avoids stale pages and protects readers from confusing intent with implementation.

## What is the current version 1.0 target?

The public planning language expects Early Access to last around one to two years, sometimes described as roughly 24 months. That is a planning horizon, not a confirmed full-release date. Scope, stability, feedback, balance, and the completion of planned systems can move the endpoint.

The direct answer is that WARDOGS has a broad Early Access roadmap but no fixed delivery calendar. More content and deeper progression are planned, while the exact schedule and values remain not listed in the collected sources.
