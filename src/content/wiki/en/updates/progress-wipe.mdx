## Will WARDOGS wipe progress after the Beta?

The official sources do not confirm a complete Beta-to-Early Access carryover policy. They explain that Cash persists from match to match and that XP progression and a longer-term metagame are planned, but they do not promise that test cash, XP, equipment, or unlocks will transfer into the Early Access product. Treat every carryover field as “待确认” until a dated announcement says otherwise.

This distinction matters because “persistent” describes the published in-match economy, not necessarily a test account’s lifetime. A Closed Beta can use temporary data while still demonstrating cross-match Cash. Do not treat a balance shown in a test screenshot as a launch advantage.

## What does persistent Cash mean?

Team17 says every player starts with a published $10,000 and that Cash persists from match to match. Each life uses that balance to buy weapons, equipment, utility, and vehicles, while team actions such as revives, transport, and objective control can reward more. The statement establishes continuity within the game’s economy, not the result of a future wipe.

Cash, XP, and Control Zone score are separate systems. Score decides the match, Cash funds the next life, and XP belongs to role-based progression. A reset in one layer would not automatically prove a reset in the others; the developer must specify the scope.

## What did the Beta recap say?

The official [Beta Recap & Next Steps](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/allnews/) announced that the Closed Beta weekend was over and pointed players toward a later announcement. It did not publish a table for Cash, XP, equipment, or account carryover. The end of the test therefore confirms the end of that service window, not a particular wipe result.

If a future announcement states that test data will be reset, record the date, affected systems, and whether the reset applies to all participants. If it says nothing, keep the page marked “待确认.” A community post cannot replace an official policy statement.

## Are seasonal resets planned?

The Early Access planning material describes a deeper seasonal metagame and discusses Cash, XP, Gold Bars, Player Skills, challenges, a Black Market, and a Vault. Those systems are planned for development during Early Access, and the public record does not provide a final seasonal calendar or retention table. A planned seasonal structure is not proof that every season will reset the same fields.

Gold Bars and cosmetics are discussed as longer-term account value in planning material, while Cash is used for per-life loadouts. Keep those layers separate when explaining a possible reset. Conversion rates, season lengths, permanent unlocks, and reward catalogues are “待确认.”

## Could equipment or unlocks be wiped separately?

Yes, that possibility cannot be ruled in or out from the collected public sources. Neither source promises that test equipment, XP-unlocked items, or account cosmetics will carry over.

Do not infer a wipe from a missing item in a test menu, either. A feature may be disabled, staged, or tied to a different entitlement. The correct page structure is a table of systems with a source and status, not one blanket “everything resets” claim.
