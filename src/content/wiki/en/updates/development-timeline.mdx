## What is the current WARDOGS Development Timeline?

The current public timeline begins with the WARDOGS reveal in February 2026, moves through closed testing in August, lists an FPS Games Show appearance on September 3, and places Steam Early Access on September 10, 2026. Version 1.0 has no fixed public date; the developer’s current planning horizon is roughly one to two years of Early Access. Dates describe the evidence available on August 27, 2026 and should be rechecked against new announcements.

The timeline separates tests from the commercial launch. A Closed Alpha or Closed Beta is not the Early Access release, and access rules can differ between each build. This page keeps those milestones in chronological order so readers do not mistake a playtest invitation or trailer for a public launch.
