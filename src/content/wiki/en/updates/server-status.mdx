## Are WARDOGS servers online?

The collected sources do not expose a live telemetry dashboard or a verified uptime endpoint, so this page cannot claim that every WARDOGS server is online right now. The launch-day official feed reported a controlled login queue and a client hotfix for players unable to join servers or unexpectedly kicked from matches. Use the official Steam announcements as the first place to look for maintenance and launch-status notices, then compare Discord and Steam discussions for account-specific or regional reports.

The absence of a new announcement is not proof that there is no problem. A queue may be operating in controlled batches, an entitlement may be missing, or a player may have a local connection issue. Treat the current state as unconfirmed until an official status message or a consistent set of reports explains the situation.

## Which sources should you check?

The official Steam Community announcement list is the highest-confidence source in the collected material. It carries dated notices about Early Access, launch queues, hotfixes, preload instructions, and server scaling. The WARDOGS Discord is a useful live community channel, but a message there still needs to be read in its announcement context rather than copied from a player chat.

Steam Community discussions can reveal whether other players see the same error or access problem. They are useful for detecting a pattern, not for proving an official outage. A single discussion thread may describe a local network, account entitlement, or test-window issue.

## How do you separate an outage from missing access?

First confirm that the Steam Early Access product is installed and that your account owns the correct entitlement. Then check whether the official announcement says the service is under maintenance, in a queue, or admitting players in batches. If other players can launch while your account cannot, the issue may be access or entitlement rather than a global outage.

During launch, the developers reported that players would be admitted in controlled batches after the client hotfix while they monitored server population. That notice explains why queue length and access could change quickly. It does not provide a permanent error fix or a current uptime guarantee.

## What should you do during a suspected outage?

Read the newest official announcement, check Discord announcements, and record the exact time and error text before restarting repeatedly. Avoid installing unofficial patches or downloading a different build. If Steam shows no Early Access entitlement, return to the official purchase page instead of treating the missing button as a server failure.

Once an official update resolves the problem, note the announcement date and affected window. If no official statement exists, label the incident as a community report and keep it separate from a confirmed outage. This approach prevents a single old beta complaint from being presented as the current Early Access status.
