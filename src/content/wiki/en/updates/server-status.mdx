## Are WARDOGS servers online?

The collected sources do not expose a live telemetry dashboard or a verified uptime endpoint, so this page cannot claim that every WARDOGS server is online right now. Use the official Steam announcements as the first place to look for maintenance and launch-status notices. Then compare the official Discord and Steam Community discussions for account-specific or regional reports.

The absence of a new announcement is not proof that there is no problem. A test may be closed, an invitation may be missing, or a player may have a local connection issue. Treat the current state as “待确认” until an official status message or a consistent set of reports explains the situation.

## Which sources should you check?

The official Steam Community announcement list is the highest-confidence source in the collected material. It carries dated notices about Early Access, Closed Beta windows, preload instructions, and server scaling. The WARDOGS Discord is a useful live community channel, but a message there still needs to be read in its announcement context rather than copied from a player chat.

Steam Community discussions can reveal whether other players see the same error or access problem. They are useful for detecting a pattern, not for proving an official outage. A single discussion thread may describe a local network, account entitlement, or test-window issue.

## How do you separate an outage from missing access?

First confirm that the game or Playtest is currently scheduled and that your Steam account owns the correct entitlement. Then check whether the official announcement says the test is open, closed, or under maintenance. If other players can launch while your account cannot, the issue may be access or entitlement rather than a global outage.

During the documented Closed Beta, the developers warned that high preorder turnout required them to analyze server scaling and stability. That context explains why test access and server capacity could change quickly. It does not provide a permanent error fix or a current uptime guarantee.

## What should you do during a suspected outage?

Read the newest official announcement, check Discord announcements, and record the exact time and error text before restarting repeatedly. Avoid installing unofficial patches or downloading a different build. If Steam shows no entitlement, return to the playtest or purchase instructions instead of treating the missing button as a server failure.

Once an official update resolves the problem, note the announcement date and affected window. If no official statement exists, label the incident as a community report and keep it separate from a confirmed outage. This approach prevents a single old beta complaint from being presented as the current Early Access status.
