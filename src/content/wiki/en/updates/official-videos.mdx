This index groups the videos by purpose: reveal, game-mode explanation, gameplay footage, Early Access development, and a candid “10 Reasons NOT to Buy” devlog. ## How should videos be used as evidence?

First identify the uploader, title, date, and whether the material is a trailer, explanation, announcement, or developer discussion. Then separate spoken claims, on-screen observations, and editorial interpretation. A spoken claim can support a high-level rule; an image can support only what is visibly present; and an inference must be labeled as a reading rather than a confirmed stat.

The safest workflow is to open the official video, cross-check the matching Steam or Team17 page, and record the build or publication context. Where subtitles are missing or automatic captions are uncertain, link the video without inventing a transcript. This keeps the video index useful to players and safe for future updates.
