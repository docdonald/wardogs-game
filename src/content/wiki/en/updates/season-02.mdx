## When does WARDOGS Season 2 start?

The official [Season 02 teaser](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/announcements/) schedules WARDOGS Season 2 for **October 15, 2026**. The teaser does not publish a launch hour, maintenance window, or complete patch note, so use the official feed for the final timing rather than treating midnight or a regional store refresh as confirmed.

This page separates the announced date from the details that are still coming. The date is useful for planning your play sessions and checking the client; it is not evidence that every rumored Season 2 feature or reset rule has shipped.

## What does Update 0.1.2 tell us about Season 2?

The September 29 Update 0.1.2 announcement says Season 2 is planned as a larger content update with new map conditions, equipment, balance reworks, and additional features. It does not name the full equipment list, map-condition rules, balance values, or the exact launch package. Treat those categories as a confirmed development scope, not a finished feature list.

Before Season 2 arrives, Update 0.1.2 also changes the current client and server flow. It fixes the Windows 11 **WD-L020** crash associated with KB5124010, addresses several cash and XP exploits, and adds a new **DEPLOY** selection flow for Official, Community, Infantry Mode, and Low-Level servers. The [server-browser guide](/guides/community-server-browser) and [crash-fix guide](/updates/crash-fix) cover the current actions.

## Is a Season 2 wipe confirmed?

The dated Season 2 teaser confirms the start date, but it does not spell out the reset scope. Earlier official Early Access planning says that a season can reset Cash and XP progression, automatically convert Cash into Gold Bars, and keep Gold Bars and cosmetics across seasons. That is the published seasonal model; it is not a Season 2 launch checklist with a final conversion rate or reset timestamp.

The [PC Gamer XP report](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/i-did-the-math-on-my-wardogs-xp-grind-and-now-i-wish-i-hadnt/) describes the community’s concern about the current grind and repeats the October 15 timing, but its interpretation cannot replace a Season 2 patch note. Keep Cash, XP, role levels, paid unlocks, Gold Bars, and cosmetics as separate fields until BULKHEAD publishes the exact scope.

## What is still not announced?

The current official posts do not list the Season 2 launch hour, maintenance duration, complete map-condition roster, equipment names, balance values, conversion rate, or a final table of what resets and what stays. They also do not confirm whether every Season 1 unlock will return in the same role track or level order.

Do not turn a teaser image, a community spreadsheet, or a secondary article into a guaranteed feature. Link each later detail to the official announcement that names the build and its scope, then update the table here when the source is published.

## How should you prepare before October 15?

Keep playing normal matches and spend Cash according to the current Season 1 goals instead of grinding a rumored unlock. If the seasonal model is applied as previously described, unspent Cash may be converted, but the official posts do not give a rate that makes a pre-wipe purchase mathematically safe. Buy gear you will actually use, preserve a cheap fallback loadout, and avoid exploit or XP-farming routes.

Make a short record of the current build, role levels, Cash, Gold Bars, and cosmetics before the update. Screenshots help you compare the account after maintenance, but they do not create a carryover promise. Check the official Steam feed again before the date for the final reset scope and launch instructions.

## What should you do when the Season 2 client arrives?

Let Steam finish the official update, restart the client if the launcher requests it, and read the current maintenance notice before joining a server. Use **DEPLOY** to choose the server category that matches your squad, and use the current browser filters rather than relying on a Season 1 screenshot. If WD-L020 or another launch error returns, record the exact message and build before changing system security settings.

The direct answer is that Season 2 is scheduled for October 15, while its full content and reset details still require the official launch patch. Use the date to plan, use Update 0.1.2 to update your client, and leave unknown values unfilled until BULKHEAD publishes them.
