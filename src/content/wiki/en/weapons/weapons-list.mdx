## What weapons are confirmed in WARDOGS?

The official public record confirms that weapons are purchased as part of a custom loadout for each life. It also names three weapon models in the Supporter Edition announcement: A-91, Bushmaster, and KH-2002, each appearing in a faction-branded rifle-camo description. Those names are confirmed as references in the announcement, not as a complete launch catalogue or a promise about their current availability.

The Steam store and Team17 page do not publish a full list of firearms, launchers, ammunition, damage, recoil, magazine size, attachment slots, or prices. This page therefore separates named official references from fields that are “待确认.” A screenshot or promotional frame cannot fill a missing specification.

## How are weapons obtained?

The official store description says each life uses cash to buy a custom loadout containing weapons, gear, utility, and vehicles. This is different from a traditional account unlock where a gun is permanently equipped after reaching a level. A player must consider both the cost of the current life and the persistent cash that remains for later matches.

The exact relationship between progression XP and weapon access is not fully published. The official FAQ discusses progression tracks and specialist items, but it does not provide a stable weapon unlock table. Unlock fees, level requirements, re-purchase prices, and an item’s place in the shop are “待确认.”

## What does the official Supporter Edition name tell us?

The Steam announcement lists a Valkyra Branded A-91 Rifle Camo, a Lonestar Branded Bushmaster Camo, and a Manticore Branded KH-2002 Camo. These lines verify the spelling of those three model names in an official cosmetic context. They do not state that the camos alter damage, faction access, handling, or weapon availability.

The same announcement says the Supporter Edition offers cosmetic content and that cash, Gold Bars, and camos are not to be directly monetized under the stated policy. Do not use the camo list to infer faction-exclusive arsenals or competitive bonuses. Any weapon role assigned from a picture remains “待确认.”

## How should new players choose a weapon?

Choose a weapon that matches the route and task of the current life. A controllable rifle may support objective presence, while an anti-vehicle tool may matter more when armor threatens a supply line. The official sources do not identify a universal best gun, so recommendations must be tied to the player’s budget and objective rather than raw power.

Keep ammunition and other utility in the purchase decision. A gun that consumes more of the balance or requires a specialist setup can be a poor first choice if the squad only needs a simple route to the Control Zone. Exact ammo types, refill behavior, and attachment costs are “待确认.”
