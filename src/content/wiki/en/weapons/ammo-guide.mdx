## What is confirmed about WARDOGS ammunition?

The official Steam page confirms that tactical gunplay is central to WARDOGS and that players buy weapons, equipment, and utility as part of each life’s loadout. The collected weapon references also describe ammunition types, ballistic behavior, weight, and handling as meaningful parts of the weapon model. They do not provide a complete official table of calibers, damage, penetration, magazine size, or supply cost.

That evidence supports an ammo guide focused on decision-making rather than invented numbers. Exact values should be read from the live build or a later official reference. Until then, any detailed “best caliber” ranking is “待确认.”

## How should you think about ammo in a loadout?

Ammo is part of the same persistent-cash decision as the weapon, equipment, utility, and vehicle you buy for a life. A high-cost loadout may give more capability but expose more of your stored balance if the life ends quickly. New players should choose a weapon they can control and leave room for the wider role their squad needs.

The public sources emphasize that every action and purchase has a consequence. This means an ammo choice should be connected to the objective, distance, expected fights, and whether you are supporting a transport or holding a position. No collected source proves a universal starter amount or a fixed resupply route.

## What do ballistics and weight change?

The sourced gunplay reference describes real-world-inspired ballistics alongside a design goal that remains playable rather than a full simulation. It also treats ammunition weight and weapon handling as separate considerations. Heavier equipment may affect how a loadout feels, but the exact movement, recoil, and stamina effects are not published in a verified numeric table.

Use those design directions as questions to test in the current build: how far you expect to shoot, how much equipment your role carries, and whether a weapon remains controllable while moving with the squad. Do not convert a trailer shot or a third-party chart into a confirmed recoil or penetration value.

## How does ammo interact with the team objective?

WARDOGS is not only a shooting gallery. Three teams fight over a Control Zone, and support actions such as revives, transport, spotting, and objective control can earn cash. A player who conserves ammunition while providing overwatch or moving supplies may contribute more than a player who spends an expensive loadout on an isolated fight.

Vehicles and destruction also change ammunition decisions. Rockets and heavy weapons can affect buildings or armored targets, while a helicopter may need to move supplies rather than seek a duel. The exact inventory limits and vehicle ammunition rules are not confirmed in the collected sources.

## What should beginners test first?

Start with one familiar weapon and observe its handling at the distances your squad actually fights. Record how often you run dry, whether the route to resupply is safe, and what the loadout costs relative to the cash you earn. Change one variable at a time so you can separate weapon feel from movement, attachment, and network conditions.

Do not spend persistent cash chasing a third-party “meta” that has no cited build version. The public material confirms the existence of a tactical weapon system, not a final balance patch. Until official numbers are released, the best answer is a repeatable test plan rather than a fabricated tier list.
