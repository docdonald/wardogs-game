#官方官网素材1
#网址：[https://www.team17.com/games/wardogs](https://www.team17.com/games/wardogs)
标题：WARDOGS — Persistent Cash Metagame
#内容
官方说明每名玩家从 $10,000 开始，每条生命购买武器、装备和载具。救援队友、把友军运输到目标区、控制目标等团队行为都会奖励现金，现金会从一场比赛保留到下一场。官方还把 Hot Zone、直升机运输、FOB 建造和炮击坦克列为不同的赚钱或支援路线，但没有公开每项固定金额。

### 原文（Team17 官网游戏页，英文原文）

> WARDOGS is an All-Out Warfare FPS that combines tactical gunplay and combined arms combat with building and destruction mechanics in a large-scale, up to 100-player militaristic sandbox.
>
> The game mode - Inspired by ‘King of The Hill’ - has three teams fight for control of a randomised 2x2km ‘Control Zone’ within a 256km² map. The team with the most players within the Control Zone earns points - the first team to reach 100 points wins the match. The variables in zone location and individual player tactics mean no match ever plays the same.
>
> PERSISTENT CASH METAGAME
>
> In WARDOGS – Every. Decision. Matters.
>
> Every player starts their WARDOG journey with $10,000. In each life, you purchase and build your loadout, choosing from a wide selection of weapons, equipment and vehicles to help your team win the match.
>
> Every teamplay action you perform rewards cash. From reviving squadmates, transporting friendlies to the zone, or controlling the objective. We don’t just encourage teamplay; we reward it. It’s the player’s choice how they want to play, earn, and spend their profits.
>
> Cash persists from match-to-match so spending big on better gear, equipment, or a vehicle at the right time has the potential to turn the tide of battle and could yield massive returns.
>
> PLAY YOUR WAY
>
> You Choose How You Want to Play & Profit.
>
> There’s no one way to play, earn and progress. Instead, WARDOGS gives players the freedom to take on the Control Zone in any number of ways. Whether you’re a lone wolf helping your team or part of a dedicated squad, how you approach the battlefield is entirely up to you.
>
> You could chase the 'Hot Zone' to earn double the cash, purchase a ghillie suit and snipe from a distance, or ferry supplies to teammates by helicopter. Additionally, you could choose to fortify a strategic chokepoint by building your own forward operating base, or if you're feeling destructive... man an artillery tank and rain down destruction on the enemy.
>
> In WARDOGS, there are no set rules of engagement. It’s ultimately your choice how you want to approach the battlefield.
>
> DESTRUCTION & BASE BUILDING
>
> Bring the Chaos to Tactical Warfare.
>
> Destroy apartment blocks with rocket launchers or tear your way through towns in heavily armoured tanks. The world around you reacts to your presence. Conversely, strategize with your teammates to build upon and fortify key locations within the map for a tactical advantage.
>
> 100% PLAYER DRIVEN SANDBOX
>
> Every Moment in WARDOGS is 100% Player Driven and tied together with local voice chat to either talk tactics with teammates or troll enemy players. This is not another ‘Battle Royale’. This is not another ‘Extraction Shooter’.
>
> WARDOGS is a new Tactical take on the All-Out Warfare FPS genre that rewards decision making, communication and teamwork – and allows you to play how you want within a modern militaristic setting.
